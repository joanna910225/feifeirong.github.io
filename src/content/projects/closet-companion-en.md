## The idea

- Use FashionCLIP and a multimodal LLM for item recognition and outfit reasoning.
- Designed an MVP scope, a deferred-closet onboarding funnel, and a seven-day go / no-go validation plan.
- Keep the first useful moment close to the in-store capture: take a photo, see a few ranked outfit directions, then add real closet items incrementally.
